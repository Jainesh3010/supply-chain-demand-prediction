How It Works

From historical supply-chain data to intelligent demand predictions.

01

Data Collection

Historical sales, inventory, pricing and supply-chain information are collected from multiple sources.

02

Data Processing

Numerical and categorical features are processed through the ML preprocessing pipeline for model readiness.

03

Machine Learning

A Random Forest Regressor learns patterns from historical demand data using ensemble decision trees.

04

Demand Prediction

The trained model predicts expected product demand for new supply-chain input data in real time.

Why Demand Prediction?

Accurate demand forecasting is the backbone of a resilient supply chain.

Better Inventory Planning

Know exactly how much stock to order — no guesswork, no waste.

Reduce Stockouts

Prevent lost sales by anticipating demand before shelves go empty.

Avoid Over-Stocking

Cut holding costs and free up capital by stocking only what you need.

Smarter Supply Chain

Data-driven decisions across procurement, logistics and distribution.
